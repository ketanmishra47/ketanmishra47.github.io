Healthcare.

Healthcare creative is bound by compliance and powered by trust. Every claim needs a citation. Every hook has to read like something a 55-year-old worried about a parent would actually believe.

I've shipped Medicare Advantage and ACA enrollment campaigns. The creative lands in tight seasonal windows. Open Enrollment opens in October. By August the hook library is ready, the VSL variants approved, the email lifecycle staged, the landing pages live.

The discipline shows up in two places: hook libraries built off angles that convert in this vertical, and structured templates that respect the audience's age and reading patience. Performance tagging means last season's winners get reused cleaner the next year.

Health Insurance.

Health insurance is the operator side of healthcare. The marketing is more compressed, the funnels more transactional. Plan comparison and premium savings drive most of the volume.

Worked on ACA enrollment campaigns where the math has to be visible in 4 seconds. Premium tax credits, deductible thresholds, network checks. The hook is the number. The proof is the math.

Output side: shorter VSLs and cleaner statics, plus calculator-style landing pages. The audience is shopping. The creative has to feel like a tool they're using to compare options.

Fintech.

Fintech in performance marketing means consumer lending and debt relief. The copy lives at the intersection of regulated language and direct response.

I've shipped Personal Loans and Debt Relief campaigns where the funnel runs from a worried Google search to a live phone call. The hook has to interrupt the doomscroll. The body has to build trust fast.

What plays here: storytelling that respects the audience's actual financial moment, and copy that clears compliance the first time. Structured formats from VSL to advertorial that all carry the same voice.

Consumer Lending.

The unsecured side of consumer finance. Personal Loans and debt consolidation. Credit repair adjacent.

The audience comes in stressed. The creative job is making the next step feel manageable. Hook libraries get built around real triggers like job loss and payment shock. VSLs get structured around the relief moment.

I've worked across DR funnels where the gap between a $200 day and a $2,000 day is one hook variant. The discipline is testing fast and scaling only what holds for a full week.

Auto Insurance.

Auto insurance sits in P&C. Quote funnels and comparison sites are the primary surfaces. Multi-policy savings is the most common hook.

The creative is shorter and more calculator-driven than other verticals. The audience is rate-shopping. Hooks lead with the dollar amount. Landing pages do the quote math.

I've shipped Auto campaigns where the test cycle moves weekly. The metric is bind rate. Creative job: feeding the funnel with hooks that hold for a few weeks before the format fatigues.
