// 02 · Hook library

One batch of 22 winning short-form ads in personal loans, broken down by the pipeline in P—004. Every hook is named, ranked by how often it won, and written as a template the next variant can start from.

Hook A13/22 ads · 59%

Comment-reply hook

“I need a loan but my credit score sucks...”

MechanismA native “reply to comment” sticker, or a user comment as a text overlay.

Why it worksIt qualifies the viewer in the first second and says what they are already thinking. The creator reads as someone helping.

Hook B5/22 ads · 23%

Generosity hook

“This woman paid for everyone's groceries in this Walmart...”

MechanismA stranger does something very generous in public.

Why it worksA wide curiosity gap. The loan becomes the thing that made a good deed possible.

Hook C4/22 ads · 18%

Cash-first hook

“$35,000 deposit was made...”

MechanismOpens on a bank deposit, a cash reveal or a shocked partner.

Why it worksRelief lands faster with this hook than with any other in the batch.

Hook D2/22 ads · 9%

“Legit or scam?” hook

Text overlay: “2 days later”

MechanismThe presenter plays a skeptic testing the trend, then cuts to “2 days later”.

Why it worksIt turns the viewer's doubt into proof. Built for audiences who have seen every ad.

Hook E2/22 ads · 9%

How-to headline hook

“How to get a loan even with bad credit”

MechanismA plain headline that matches what the viewer would type into search.

Why it worksIt matches search intent word for word, so it asks the least of the viewer.

Hook F1/22 ads · 5%

Family hook

“Hey mom, I just deposited $50,000 into your account.”

MechanismMoney arrives as a gift to someone the viewer loves.

Why it worksIt turns a financial product into a gift. The strongest emotional pull in the batch.

Some ads open with 2 hooks, so the shares add up past 100%. Ad file names removed.
