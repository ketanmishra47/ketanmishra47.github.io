P—010 Leadership Ongoing

Cross-vertical creative ops.

5 verticals, one creative org. Built Creo, a custom internal tool, to break the silos. Hooks and angles travel across, and performance lands in a leadership-readable view.

Built forAdsparkx · 5 verticals
FormatOperating model
StatusOngoing

// Context

5 verticals were running in silos. Each had its own creative strategist and its own hook library. What worked in Personal Loans never made it to Auto Insurance. What worked in Medicare never made it to ACA. Performance compounded inside each silo and stopped at the wall.

Leadership saw the verticals as 5 separate creative orgs. There was no creative-first view of what was actually performing across the company. The bet: performance could double if working hooks and angles traveled across silos.

// Approach

The model has four moving parts.

Org shape. 5 creative strategists, 1 per vertical, each owning their hook library and audience knowledge. 5 editors in a combined workflow so any vertical can pull from any editor. 1 media buying lead overseeing multiple media buyers across the verticals. The strategists stay vertical-deep. The editors stay vertical-agnostic.

Shared briefing and taxonomy. Briefs route the same way across all 5 verticals. Performance taxonomy is identical: same hook types, body structures, CTA patterns, fatigue curves. A winning hook in one vertical gets named and indexed in the shared library, then tested in another.

Vertical hook libraries. Each vertical maintains its own hook library built off its winners. The libraries sit in one place and use the shared taxonomy. A strategist in Auto Insurance can pull a Personal Loans hook, see the structural pattern, and adapt it to their audience without rebuilding from scratch.

Daily cadence with weekly sharing rhythm. Each vertical ships daily. The unified rhythm is the cross-vertical share: what's working this week and what's worth adapting across. Performance data feeds back into the shared dashboard (P—001).

// Outcome

The silos broke. A hook winning in Medicare gets adapted to ACA the same week. A creative angle proven in Personal Loans shows up in Debt Relief variants the next sprint.

Leadership got a creative-first view of org-level performance. Cross-vertical comparison became possible through the shared taxonomy. A winning hook in one vertical could be named in the library and reused in another without losing its structural pattern.

Hooks travel across silos. Performance compounds at the org level.
