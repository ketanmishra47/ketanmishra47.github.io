// Context

Vibelets needed organic traffic. The lead acquisition infrastructure (see P—008) was running, but the blog was the surface that pulled in search traffic at scale. Posts had to rank in Google, rank in AI search, read like the brand, and route readers into the lead-magnet flow.

That meant four jobs in one. Brand-voice content. SEO that ranked in Google. Generative-engine optimization for AI search surfaces. Lead-magnet routing on every post so traffic landed somewhere useful.

// Approach

The work runs across four layers, each with its own tooling.

Brand-voice authoring. Same voice playbook as P-008's content stack work. A Vibelets brand guide locks the tone, vocabulary, structural moves, and the takes the brand never makes. A Claude workflow trained on the guide drafts in-voice. The editor pass focuses on sharpening the argument.

Research pipeline. Perplexity handles depth queries when a post needs sourced facts, real numbers, current state of a topic, or competitive context. An n8n newsletter automation pulls in industry content from across the space, feeding the angle queue. Both sources surface material the next draft pulls from.

SEO. Manual. SimilarWeb for competitor landscape. SEMrush for keyword gaps. Google's Keyword Planner for search volume. Google Trends for momentum. Every post gets a keyword target before drafting.

GEO (generative-engine optimization). Posts are structured to rank inside AI search surfaces too. Clear claims, traceable sources, structural markers, and answer-ready paragraphs. The same content that ranks in Google ends up cited in answers from Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI surfaces.
