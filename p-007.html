P—007 Copy Ongoing

Founder-led LinkedIn growth.

Ghostwriting practice for founders who shouldn't be at a keyboard. Voice DNA per client, fed by an n8n news pipeline. Daily posts batched weekly.

Built forDusan Radovanovic + Nipun Taneja
FormatGhostwriting practice
StatusActive

// Context

Founders need LinkedIn presence. They also need their hands free to build product. The work to maintain a cadence, hold a consistent voice, pick which threads to engage on, and ride news in real time is full-time. Most founders don't have that time.

Generic ghostwriting fails because founders see ghostwritten content from across the table every week. The day someone in their network smells it, the credibility is gone. The fix: write in the founder's actual voice. Cadence, vocabulary, the takes they reach for unprompted, even the typos they make under speed.

// Approach

The practice has four reusable components. Each one runs across founders. The inputs change per client.

Voice DNA doc. Built first, per founder. Multi-hour interviews capture cadence, references, vocabulary, the takes they reach for unprompted, the things they'd never say. Every post gets written against it.

News intel pipeline. An n8n workflow tracks recent happenings across AI and the founder's adjacent space. Surfaces material to post on. Drafts get written off the intel, then run through the voice rules.

Comment scaling. Engagement gets the post attention. Keywords trigger response variations through ManyChat-style automation where LinkedIn allows. Where it doesn't, manual responses with a tracking spreadsheet so nothing gets dropped.

Cadence. One post per day per founder. Routine slots batched weekly on Mondays. Mid-week posts opportunistic when news breaks.

// The founders

// 01

Dusan Radovanovic

hookandscale.io

Brought in to scale Dusan's LinkedIn presence off the back of hookandscale.io. He started the partnership at 5K followers, looking for distribution to grow his offer.

Style rules: hooks-and-numbers discipline at the open, plus build-in-public framing where his own work was the proof. The n8n news intel pipeline kept him posting on what was breaking in AI, in his voice, before anyone else moved on it.

Followers 5K → 25K+
// 02

Nipun Taneja

Vibelets · vibelets.ai

Brought in to drive signups to vibelets.ai through LinkedIn and long-form blog distribution. The voice DNA followed the same playbook as Dusan: hooks-and-numbers discipline plus build-in-public framing. Post types stayed in the same two lanes: build-in-public and news-mixing.

The mechanic that scaled differently: every post carried a CTA to drop a comment word that triggered a DM with the vibelets.ai lead magnet. To receive it, recipients first connected with Nipun, compounding follower growth on top of signups.

The other addition: Claude + n8n automated the content layer. A skills file trained on Nipun's writing tone meant drafts arrived in-voice without a fresh human pass every time.

Cadence ran at 5 posts per week, batched on the same weekly rhythm as the rest of the practice.

Followers 1.2K → 3K+

// Outcome

Both founders shipped LinkedIn presence without writing themselves. The cadence held weekly. The voice held across thousands of posts.

Dusan went from 5K to 25K+ followers while the practice ran. Growth tracked his offer launch, and the news intel pipeline kept him surfing the AI conversation in real time.

Nipun went from 1.2K to 3K+ followers alongside the LinkedIn-and-blog flow into vibelets.ai signups. The lead-magnet comment trigger turned engagement into both connections and product signups in the same motion.

The practice ran. Founders kept building product.
