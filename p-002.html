ClickUp + n8n creative ops.

One master n8n workflow on ClickUp. Routes 100+ creative handoffs every day and keeps thousands of assets organized across verticals.

FormatClickUp + n8n master workflow
StatusActive · 100+ deliveries / day

// Context

Two problems were eating the team. Handoffs broke daily, and scale broke the rest.

On the handoff side: scripts sat in inboxes and approvals never pinged. Status never made it back to the writer who needed it. Every step depended on a human remembering to ping the next person.

On the scale side: thousands of creatives shipping across 5 verticals with no shared naming, no shared location. Finding the right asset became a 20-minute archaeology dig. Reusing winners was impossible because nobody could find them.

// Approach

Built one master n8n workflow on top of ClickUp. The whole creative ops layer runs through this single flow. Massive at scale: 30+ branches handle every event in the creative pipeline. Each branch fires on a specific ClickUp trigger and handles one specific job: video editor assignment, custom field updates, attachment processing, validation routing, naming enforcement.

The second discipline: a shared naming convention enforced across the whole library. Every creative gets a structured filename (date + vertical + offer + variant) that makes it findable. Combined with ClickUp's grouping by status and quarter (WINNER Q3'24, WINNER Q4'24, and so on), the team can pull any winning asset from any quarter in seconds.

// Inside the system

// Outcome

The system handles 100+ creative deliveries every day, 365 days a year. Briefs route, approvals trigger, attachments process, status updates write themselves. Handoff overhead disappears.

The library scale stopped being a problem. Any winning asset from any quarter is findable in seconds. Winners get reused because nobody has to dig for them.

Ops layer ran the team. The team ran the creatives.
