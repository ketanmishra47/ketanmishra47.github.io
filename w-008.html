Questions + answers

What is Vibelets.ai? Vibelets.ai is an AI-powered marketing platform that helps you create, launch and optimize Facebook (Meta) campaigns with speed and precision. It unifies creative generation, campaign setup, and performance insights in one place.

Who is Vibelets built for? Vibelets is designed for D2C brands, startups, growth teams and performance marketers who want to launch high-performing ads quickly without navigating complex ad manager interfaces.

Which advertising platforms does Vibelets support? At launch, Vibelets supports Facebook (Meta) end-to-end — from creative creation to campaign launch and live monitoring. Integrations with TikTok, Google and other platforms are coming soon.

How does Vibelets improve campaign performance? Vibelets uses AI to analyse performance in real time, detect opportunities or issues, and recommend the next best action — helping you scale winners and control wasted spend effortlessly.

Can I create and manage campaigns inside Vibelets? Yes. You can build and launch campaigns directly in Vibelets and take key actions like pause, launch, clone, replicate based on AI guidance. Edit functionality is coming soon.

Does Vibelets help with creative development? Absolutely. Vibelets generates high-quality brand images, ad creatives and platform-ready video ads using AI.

What analytics does Vibelets provide? Vibelets gives you a complete performance view of your Meta campaigns — including spend, conversions, ROAS, CTR, CPC, CAC and other key metrics — all in a simple, unified dashboard.

How real-time is the data inside Vibelets? Campaign data is synced through Meta’s official APIs, giving you accurate, near real-time insights for better decision-making.
