VSL & long-form video.

VSLs are the workhorse of performance copy in regulated verticals. They have to hold a stressed audience for 8 to 20 minutes while building trust and clearing compliance line by line.

I've shipped VSLs across Personal Loans, Debt Relief, Medicare Advantage, and ACA. The script discipline: every VSL is built off a hook library and a structural template that holds across variants. Every variant carries a performance tag so what wins gets reused.

Long-form video extends the same logic into hosted explainers and advertorial-tone walk-throughs. The structure is portable. The voice is what carries.

UGC video.

UGC sits at the front of every modern performance funnel. Real-feeling video designed to earn 3 seconds of attention before the hook does the work.

I've worked with UGC creator pools across all 5 verticals. The job is briefing right. Hook libraries the creator pulls from, and performance tagging by creator and by format. The post-production layer keeps everything on one production rhythm.

UGC plays differently than scripted. The discipline lives in the creator brief and the post-production rhythm.

Static & display.

The oldest format still doing the heaviest lifting in performance feeds. Image ads, carousels, banners, and social statics.

The discipline is hook-as-headline. Visual interrupts in 1 second. Text qualifies in 5 words. Variants cycle daily.

I've shipped static creative across all 5 verticals. The framework: hook libraries paired to visual templates, batch-tested weekly, winners scaled, fatigued retired.

Native advertising.

Native is the format that doesn't look like advertising. Article-style placements and advertorial pages dominate the surface.

I've shipped native across consumer finance and health verticals. The discipline is editorial-voice copy that earns the click before it makes the pitch. The format hides the marketing math inside the storytelling.

Native runs in parallel to VSL traffic. Hook discipline carries from VSL. The surface is what changes.

Short-form video.

Short-form is where attention lives. TikTok and Reels are the primary surfaces. 15 to 60 seconds to land a hook and build enough trust to move someone.

I've worked with short-form across UGC creator pools and AI-gen pipelines. The script discipline is brutal: every word has to earn its second. The hook lands or the viewer scrolls.

The newest format with the highest learning curve. Hook discipline carries from VSL. The execution is a different production rhythm.
