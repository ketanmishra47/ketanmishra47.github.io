W—013 Email sequence

Vibelets: 4-email free-trial onboarding sequence

A 4-email free-trial onboarding sequence for vibelets.ai: one job per email, sent on what the user has or hasn't done, with the reasoning behind each one.

Length848 words · 4 min read

A free-trial onboarding sequence for vibelets.ai, the AI that plans and runs Meta ads for D2C brands with the owner approving every move. 4 emails, each with one job: get the trial user to the next moment where the product proves itself.

The sequence at a glance

EmailSendsOne jobSubject
01On signupConnect the Meta ad accountYour first plan is 2 minutes away
02When the Baseline Report is readyApprove the first moveHere's what your ads did last month
03Day 3, if a plan is approvedReview the first creativesYour next ads are drafted
042 days before the trial endsPick a planKeep the plan running

2 rules shape the whole sequence. Every email asks for one click, and every email is triggered by what the user has or hasn't done. A user who never connects an account gets the email 01 reminder below and never sees email 02.

Email 01On signup · Goal: connect Meta
Subject

Your first plan is 2 minutes away

Preview

Connect your ad account and Vibelets starts reading.

Body

Hi first name,

Welcome to Vibelets.

Before it can plan anything, Vibelets needs to see what your ads have been doing. That takes one step: connect your Meta ad account.

Once you do, it reads your campaigns and builds your Baseline Report: what's working and what's tired. Your first weekly plan comes from that report.

Nothing runs without you. Vibelets suggests every move, and you approve it.

[Connect my ad account →]

The Vibelets team

If not connected after 24 hours

Subject: Still a guess?

Right now, your next ad is a guess. Connect your Meta account and Vibelets will tell you which ad is working, which one is tired and what to test next. It takes 2 minutes. [Connect now →]

Why this email

A trial user gets no value until the account is connected, so the welcome email asks for that and nothing else. It promises the Baseline Report as the reward and answers the biggest fear about AI touching an ad account in one line: nothing runs without you.

Email 02Baseline Report ready · Goal: first approval
Subject

Here's what your ads did last month

Preview

Your Baseline Report is ready, with a plan for next week.

Body

Hi first name,

Vibelets has read your account. Your Baseline Report is ready.

Start with 2 parts:

What's working, and why. Your best ads, ranked, with the reason each one is pulling its weight.

Next week's plan. The moves Vibelets suggests: scale the winning hook, pause the tired ads, raise budget on the winner, test a new angle. Each move shows why it's there.

Open the plan and approve the first move you agree with. That's how the loop starts: plan, run, learn, then a smarter plan next week.

[See my report →]

The Vibelets team

Why this email

It's triggered by the report, so it lands when there's something to look at. The subject names the user's own ads, the one topic every owner opens. It asks for one small yes, a single approved move, because the first approval is when a trial user starts trusting the product.

Email 03Day 3 · Goal: review creatives
Subject

Your next ads are drafted

Preview

Built from what's already working in your account.

Body

Hi first name,

Your plan called for new creatives, so Vibelets drafted them.

They're built from your account: the hooks that already won and the angle your plan wants to test. Each one comes with a note on why it should work.

Open them, keep the ones you like and skip the rest. When you approve one, it's ready to launch.

[Review my new ads →]

The Vibelets team

Why this email

Creative is the second half of the product, and the part most trial users never find on their own. Saying the ads are built from the user's own winners separates them from generic AI ads. Nothing launches until the owner approves it, so they stay in control.

Email 042 days before trial ends · Goal: pick a plan
Subject

Keep the plan running

Preview

Your trial ends on date. Here's what carries on.

Body

Hi first name,

Your trial ends on date.

So far Vibelets has read your account, built your plan and drafted your ads. Each week from here starts from what the last one taught it.

Pick the plan that matches how you run ads:

Starter, $49/month. One brand, one buyer. Your weekly plan and one ad account.

Max, $99/month. Several accounts, with approvals that need an owner. Up to 5 ad accounts and Creative Studio.

Pro, $199/month. Agencies and brands running many accounts under one set of rules.

[Choose my plan →]

The Vibelets team

Why this email

It recaps what the user has already got before it asks for money, and it names each plan by the buyer it's for, the same way the pricing page does, so each reader finds themselves in one line.
