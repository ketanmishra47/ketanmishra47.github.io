Teams.

The 5 creative functions I've built and led. A creative org that ships at performance speed.

Copywriters.

Copy is where most performance creative lives or dies. The team ships hooks, scripts, and lifecycle copy across multiple verticals every week.

I've built copy teams from junior writers up. The discipline: voice docs per vertical, hook libraries everyone pulls from, structured templates that respect production speed. Onboarding cuts a writer's ramp time from quarters to weeks.

The output: copy that performs without the senior writer rewriting every line.

Creative Strategists.

Creative strategists are the translation layer between performance data and creative direction. They look at what's working, name why, and brief the next variant.

I've led strategists across 5 verticals. The discipline: shared performance taxonomy, weekly intel rhythms, and creative briefs that compress 12 hours of analysis into a 1-page brief.

When strategy and copy run on the same rhythm, the org ships sharper creative.

Video Editors.

Editors are downstream of every winning hook. The team turns approved scripts into shipped video at performance speed.

I've worked with editor teams on VSLs, UGC cuts, and short-form. The discipline: structured project files, asset libraries, and an editing brief format that ships a script from copywriter to editor in one document.

The output: video that doesn't bottleneck on the editing stage.

Researchers.

Research is the function most teams underbuild. Without it, copy is guessing.

I've built and managed research functions covering competitor scraping, market intel, customer language libraries, and angle freshness. Most of the work runs on AI agents now, with humans curating and synthesizing.

The output: copy that lands because the angle is right. Talent matters less when research is right.

// 05

Creative Ops.

Ops is the function that lets the rest of the org actually ship. Without it, briefs sit, reviews stack, performance never gets back to the writer.

I've built creative ops layers using ClickUp and n8n. Briefs route automatically. Performance data syncs back to creative tickets. Status updates write themselves.

When ops runs, the team ships at twice the cadence with half the meetings.
